How I teach

Start with wonder

Children learn best when they explore first. I begin units with a question, a story or something to touch, and build the skills from there.

Plan across weeks

Lessons shouldn't sit on their own. I sequence learning so reading, maths and environmental studies build on each other through a shared theme.

Watch, then adapt

Observation tells me what a child is ready for. I adjust pace, materials and support, and use assessments to decide what to reteach or extend.
